Design that

FMBytex exists for one reason: too many excellent businesses are invisible online because their digital presence doesn't reflect what they actually deliver. We close that gap with confident, intentional design.

We designed and built Mortea, a bilingual beauty discovery platform, and created the brand identity and website for Maison Malak, a Montréal head spa — both live in our portfolio. Every project gets the same conviction: your digital presence should be as strong as the work you do.
