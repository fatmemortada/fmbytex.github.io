How much does a website
cost in Montreal?

Real 2026 price ranges, what actually drives the cost, and how to get an exact quote for your project within 24 hours.

The short answer

A professional small-business website in Montreal costs $1,500–$5,000 in 2026. A booking platform, marketplace, or custom web application runs $5,000–$15,000+, depending on functionality. Below is exactly what sits behind those numbers — so you can budget with confidence and spot an overpriced (or suspiciously cheap) quote.

What drives the price up (or down)

  • Custom design vs template. A template is cheaper; a custom design converts better and looks like nobody else. Every FMBytex project is custom-designed.
  • Functionality. Contact forms are cheap. Online booking, payments, client accounts, and dashboards each add real development time.
  • Content. Professional copywriting, photography, and translation (EN/FR is standard in Montréal) are skilled work — budget for them.
  • SEO setup. Technical SEO, schema markup, and keyword structure from day one cost a little now and save a lot later. See our SEO services.
  • Support after launch. Updates, backups, and improvements. Ask what's included before you sign.

Red flags in a quote

  • No timeline or vague delivery dates.
  • No mention of who writes the content.
  • "Unlimited revisions" with no process behind it.
  • A price far below the ranges above — corners are being cut somewhere (usually SEO, mobile, or speed).
